About Me

Sai works with business data to turn raw information into reports, dashboards and decisions leadership can act on. His day-to-day sits at the intersection of MIS reporting, sales performance analysis and lead management — tracking how prospects move through a pipeline, where revenue is coming from, and what the numbers say before someone has to ask.

He builds and maintains reporting using Power BI, DAX, Excel, SQL and Google Sheets, working directly with CRM data to keep lead, payment and revenue tracking accurate. That includes writing DAX measures for KPI dashboards, cleaning and modeling raw exports, and preparing the daily, weekly and monthly MIS reports that sales and management teams rely on.

Comfortable moving between spreadsheets and dashboards, he pays close attention to data accuracy — validating records, reconciling payment status, and flagging inconsistencies before they reach a report.
